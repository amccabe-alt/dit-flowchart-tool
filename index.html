BTEC Exam Guidance & Hints

Student & Teacher Guide

1. Move Shapes: Click and drag any shape around the canvas freely.

2. Edit Text Labels: Double-click on any shape text box to type custom BTEC keywords.

3. Connect Arrows: Click "Connect Shapes Mode" ON, then click the First Shape followed by the Second Shape to draw a dynamic arrow.

4. Delete Items: Click a shape or connector line and press Delete or click its remove button.

5. Scaffolding Support: Use "Load Starter Shapes" if you need a scaffolded framework, or toggle Dyslexia fonts / Text-to-Speech in Accessibility options!
