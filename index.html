Solution Guidance & BTEC Exam Hints

Student & Teacher Guide

1. Dynamic Auto-Resizing Shapes: Click inside any shape text box to type. Shapes automatically expand as text grows!

2. Keyword Bank Drag & Drop: Drag keywords from the bank directly onto any shape on the canvas, or select a shape and click a keyword chip.

3. Draw & Label Arrows: Turn Connect Mode ON, then click the source shape followed by the target shape. Click any arrow label to edit text (e.g., 'Yes'/'No' or Data Flow names).

4. Intelligent BTEC Assessor: Click Mark My Diagram to evaluate your diagram against official BTEC DIT Component 1 & 3 standards (shape selection, arrow connections, AND text keywords typed inside shapes).

5. SEN Scaffolding Options: Toggle Dyslexia-friendly fonts, High Contrast Mode, Shape Labels, or click "Load Scaffold" for starter shapes.
